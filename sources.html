§ A. CONVENTIONS & GEOMETRIC RULES

CANONICAL MAP S² → ℂP¹

Every coordinate, state vector and gate in this simulator follows the conventions below, the same ones used by the references in § B.

SYMBOL / FORMULATION MEANING & CONVENTION
|ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + eiφ sin(θ/2)|1⟩Pure state: any normalised qubit state, up to an unobservable global phase, is a point on the unit sphere.
θ ∈ [0, π]Polar angle: measured from the North pole +Z (|0⟩) toward the South pole −Z (|1⟩). The Hilbert-space angle is θ/2.
φ ∈ [0, 2π)Azimuth: measured counter-clockwise in the equatorial plane, starting at +X (|+⟩) and passing +Y (|i⟩). It is the relative phase between |0⟩ and |1⟩.
+X = |+⟩, +Y = |i⟩, +Z = |0⟩Axis states: |±⟩ = (|0⟩ ± |1⟩)/√2, |±i⟩ = (|0⟩ ± i|1⟩)/√2. Each pair sits at opposite ends of its axis.
r = (⟨X⟩, ⟨Y⟩, ⟨Z⟩)Bloch vector: its components are the expectation values of the Pauli matrices. |r| = 1 for pure states, |r| < 1 inside the ball for mixed ones.
Rn(α) = exp(−iα n·σ/2)Gates are turns: a rotation of the sphere by α about the unit axis n, right-handed. Named gates equal such a turn up to a global phase (e.g. X = i·Rx(π)).
P(±n) = (1 ± r·n) / 2Measurement: the chance of each outcome along an axis. After one shot the state is the pole that was found.
Tr ρ² = (1 + |r|²) / 2Purity: 1 for pure states on the surface, ½ for the fully mixed state ½I at the centre.

HOW TO READ THESE

Numbers in square brackets, such as [1], in the lab and in the story point to the references listed on this page.

GEOMETRIC ANGLE CAUTION

Orthogonal states (⟨0|1⟩ = 0) are 180° apart on the sphere, because the sphere doubles the Hilbert-space angle θ/2.

MEASUREMENT INTERPRETATION

A measurement along any unit axis n asks "+n or −n?". For a spin-½ particle this is a Stern–Gerlach magnet turned toward n; for other qubits the axis is abstract.
